# OriginOrbit opening film — Mars clay to flower

## Locked concept

One astronaut gathers one piece of orange clay on Mars, follows it into open space, then shapes it by hand into a five-petal flower. The astronaut **never enters the station**. Its circular door stays closed in every shot. One long, cream, ribbed oxygen hose runs from the astronaut's backpack to an exterior port on the station. The clay, hose, suit, big geometric OriginOrbit logo eyes, hand colors, and station must remain consistent. The clay flower is visibly handmade, not grown or magically conjured.

Produce **two eight-second, 16:9 clips** in Flow, each with start and end frames. The second clip starts with the **exact same image** that ends the first clip. No crossfade is needed at the join. Use the highest source quality Flow allows. The frames are 1672 × 941; an exported 720p clip is still a 720p source, regardless of the frame resolution.

The station is a visual landmark, **not a destination to enter**. In the middle of clip 1, it comes into view as the camera follows the astronaut and clay upward. In clip 2 it remains behind the astronaut, door shut.

## Review before replacing the website film

Inspect both clips frame by frame, especially the join and hand motions. Reject a take if the door opens, the astronaut enters, the hose disconnects or multiplies, the eyes change, the clay jumps between shapes, or the flower appears without visible sculpting. Export the two original clips separately; preserve their uncompressed originals for editing. After acceptance, join at the shared frame, trim any duplicated hold, then derive desktop and mobile web assets and retime the website captions to the final cut.
