# Origin & Orbit — clay film exploration

## Concept

**A raw idea takes shape.** The astronaut finds one lump of clay on Mars, carries it into weightlessness, and works that same material into a bold handmade bloom. The transformation comes from visible hands, pressure, folds, and texture. Space changes the working conditions; the clay remains the subject. This is a concept study, not a replacement for the live film yet.

The astronaut is a cream clay character with a brown opaque visor, an orange left glove, a cobalt blue right glove, and a cream backpack. The palette is near-black space, terracotta Mars clay, cream, and a restrained cobalt accent. The bloom is a sculptural flower-like object made from clay, not a natural flower.
