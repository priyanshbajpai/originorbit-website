# OriginOrbit
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Present Priyansh Bajpai's independent, AI-native creative studio and help prospective clients understand its services and start a project or book a conversation.

## Users
Founders and marketing teams in India and globally that need imagery, film, interactive experiences, or connected campaigns shaped with clear creative direction. The studio is open to suitable consumer, B2B, industrial, and technology briefs.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Solo founder; no client portfolio supplied. Services: Images & Visual Worlds; Films & Motion; Interactive Experiences; Campaigns & Creative Direction. Ongoing creative partnership is a collaboration model. No fabricated client logos, testimonials, team, or commercial outcomes.

## Brand Commitments
OriginOrbit. The founder-approved Gemini Neo-Pixel vector wordmark and OO mark bring a precise digital counterpoint to the dark space film, warm cream, terracotta, cobalt, and playful handmade clay artwork. The astronaut remains the flagship visual for V1. The founder introduction belongs after the offering, never in the hero.

## Evidence on Hand
Two user-supplied ten-second astronaut videos, now combined and optimized for desktop and mobile. Founder resume supports venture building, creative operations, and AI implementation. User-provided contact destinations: `contact@originorbit.studio`, a Google Form, and a Google Calendar appointment page.

## Open Decisions
Public deployment and domain connection. Google controls form responses and appointment availability. A later film concept is exploratory and is not part of V1.
