# Original clay artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Original PNGs are in artwork-source/; alpha-preserving optimized WebP derivatives are in public/clay/. User references informed tactile plasticine and sculpture composition; the reference images themselves are not used as website assets.

## Shared art direction
Matte, handmade plasticine with rounded chunky forms, fine fingerprints and small dents. Warm upper-left soft key light. Three-quarter view. Cream ivory, Mars orange and cobalt accents. Transparent backgrounds and generous edges. No lettering, watermark, glossy CGI, star decorations or unrelated scenery.

## Prompt set
Each subject was generated as its own asset, using this shared direction and the existing film poster as a material/identity reference. The four service subjects also used the supplied clay icon reference for handmade material, with the studio palette replacing its pastels.

1. direction: a cream circular clay navigation compass, oversized orange arrow pointing upper right, four small cobalt tick marks and a cobalt pebble. No numbered dial or glass.
2. worlds: a connected miniature sculpture, thick cream arch on an orange clay island, imaginative four-petal orange flower with cobalt centre within the arch, one cobalt sphere, no botanical stem or leaves.
3. stories: a thick cream browser-window sculpture, cobalt header with three ivory dots, orange play triangle and a folded orange clay ribbon emerging through its lower edge. No text or actual software interface.
4. ongoing: a cobalt astronaut glove from a cream suit cuff, carefully adjusting an abstract Mars-orange clay curl on a cream plinth. No realistic anatomical hand.
5. astronaut-wave: same cream cratered helmet, opaque brown visor, stocky suit, cream backpack and boots, chest buttons; character-right glove orange and character-left glove cobalt. Standing in a welcoming three-quarter pose, orange glove waving. Full-body transparent cutout.
6. astronaut-maker: same character floating with bent knees, holding a small orange sculptural flower with cobalt centre, no stem, scenery or tether. Full-body transparent cutout.
7. films: a cream handmade film camera/projector with one large cobalt reel, one small orange reel and an orange film strip curling from the lens. This additional service sculpture was made after the service menu was refined.

The live service mapping is: worlds → Images & Visual Worlds; films → Films & Motion; stories → Interactive Experiences; direction → Campaigns & Creative Direction. The ongoing glove sculpture remains an original exploratory asset, available for future collaboration material. The astronaut-maker appears in the studio section; astronaut-wave remains an exploratory pose and is not placed in the final page.

## Process icon set
Generated with the built-in image-generation tool as one transparent 2 × 2 sprite, then cropped into four independent icons in `public/clay/process/`:

1. Understand: cream magnifying glass examining an orange idea dot.
2. Define: cream clay plan with cobalt pencil and orange mark.
3. Create: two cream clay hands shaping an orange flower-like form.
4. Deliver: a finished orange and cobalt sculpture on a cream plinth.

Prompt: “Create a transparent 2 × 2 sprite of four isolated, centered handmade polymer-clay process icons for Understand, Define, Create and Deliver, each in an equal cell with generous gutters. Cream ivory, Mars orange and cobalt blue only; three-quarter view, tactile fingerprints, soft studio light, no text and no background.” The original generated sheet is preserved as `artwork-source/process-icons-sprite.png`.

## Character notes
The new illustrations consistently assign orange to the astronaut's right glove and cobalt to the left. The two supplied films contain pose/glove continuity differences; new illustrations do not alter those originals. Cream material and opaque visor keep the character distinct from a real person. This is the studio mascot, not a founder portrait.
